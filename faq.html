Common Questions

What is included in the subscription?

Unlimited access to all prompt categories (blogs, socials, ads, emails, product copy, support replies, image generation). New prompts are added weekly.

How do I get the prompts?

After subscribing, you receive a member‑only login where you can browse, copy, and download prompts in Markdown or plain text.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes – no contracts. Cancel via your account dashboard; you retain access until the end of the paid period.

Do you offer refunds?

We provide refunds for accidental or duplicate charges within 7 days of purchase. Contact support for assistance.

Is there a free tier?

We offer a limited set of 5 free preview prompts (see the Sample Prompts page). Full library requires a paid subscription.
